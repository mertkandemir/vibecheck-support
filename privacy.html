Privacy Policy

Clear information about how VibeCheck is designed to handle information when you explore and share quizzes.

1. Who operates VibeCheck?

VibeCheck is operated by Mert Kandemir (independent developer). For privacy questions, contact mertkandemir01@icloud.com.

Effective date: November 12, 2026.

2. Information involved in using the app

VibeCheck is designed to let you try entertainment quizzes without mandatory account registration. Depending on the released features, information may include:

  • Quiz selections, answers, outcomes, and basic usage events.
  • An anonymous app authentication identifier where guest sessions are enabled.
  • Language and app preferences.
  • Technical diagnostics needed to investigate errors or abuse, if enabled.
  • Advertising identifiers, consent choices, and ad interaction data only if advertising services are enabled.
  • Information you choose to send when requesting support.

Do not submit sensitive personal information in quiz answers or support messages.

3. Why information is used

Information may be processed to deliver quiz experiences and results, remember preferences, protect the service, respond to requests, and measure product reliability. If advertising is activated, relevant data may be processed for displaying and measuring ads subject to applicable consent and platform settings.

4. Service providers and disclosures

The project uses Supabase infrastructure for its backend and guest authentication capabilities. Google AdMob is configured for future advertising; production ad serving is currently disabled in project settings. Advertising functionality is not currently active in the application. If enabled later, consent choices are requested through Google’s consent management flow where applicable, and iOS tracking permission is requested separately when required. You may review advertising privacy choices in the app.

Information may be shared with processors that operate these services as required for their functions, or when legally required. We do not claim that advertising data is never shared.

5. Sharing quiz results

If you use the share feature, you decide whether and where to send your result. Sharing opens your device's native share interface; the destination service has its own privacy practices. Avoid sharing results containing information you would prefer to keep private.

6. Storage, retention and security

Data may be stored using Supabase services. Access restrictions and security policies are part of the application architecture. Retention varies by record and operational requirements. You can request deletion using the contact address below; backup copies may persist until their normal expiry. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

You may stop using the app, manage device-level permissions, and request access, correction, or deletion of personal data where applicable. In the app, open Privacy Policy and select Delete my account to remove the active guest account and its associated quiz records. Anonymous usage may limit our ability to identify a particular session without supporting information. To request deletion, email the address below with the subject “VibeCheck Data Request”. Provide information sufficient to identify the relevant session where possible; we may ask for verification before processing.

Contact: mertkandemir01@icloud.com.

8. Children and age considerations

VibeCheck quizzes are for entertainment, not psychological or medical assessment. Do not submit sensitive information in quiz or support interactions.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as features, providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes should be communicated through an appropriate channel, and the effective date updated.
