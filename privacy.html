Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026.

1. Operator and contact

VibeCheck is operated by Mert Kandemir, an independent developer. For privacy, support or deletion requests, contact mertkandemir01@icloud.com.

2. Information we process

VibeCheck works without a traditional account. When you use online features we create an anonymous guest identifier and store: quiz answers and results; shared quiz sessions, invitation codes and your answers within them; and, if you use match chat, the messages you send, emoji reactions, blocks, reports and your acceptance of the community terms. For Couple Match extra packs we store your choice, a verified rewarded-ad record (sent to us by Google AdMob with your anonymous identifier and the shared quiz id) and, if you buy one, the store transaction id and product. We never receive your payment details; payments are handled by Apple or Google. Your language choice is stored only on your device.

3. Couple Match (dating)

Couple Match is for adults (18+) and requires signing in with Apple or Google. We receive only an account identifier from them; your name and e-mail are not shown to anyone. If you use Sign in with Apple, we also keep a token issued by Apple only so we can revoke that sign-in when you delete your account. Your Couple Match profile contains a nickname, avatar, date of birth, gender, the genders and age range you want to meet and whether to show your age. Your gender and preference can reveal your sexual orientation, so we only use them, with your explicit consent, to find matches; they are never shown to other users. Instead of photos, the people you are matched with see your vibe card: nickname, avatar, your age if you allow it, up to three interests you pick from a fixed list and — unless you switch it off — your latest Discover Yourself results. No one else can see it. While you search or play, we process a short-lived online status and your quiz progress so you can be matched live; we also remember who you were matched with so you never meet the same person twice. If you allow notifications, we store a device push token and your app language to tell you about chat invitations, replies and new messages; notifications never contain message text and are delivered through Expo, Apple and Google.

4. Chat messages

Chat messages are held in a server queue only until the recipient’s device confirms it has stored them; the message text is then removed from the queue. A restricted copy is kept for 30 days solely so that reports can be verified, then deleted. Delivered messages are kept on the devices of the two participants. If a message is reported, a copy is kept as moderation evidence for up to 12 months.

5. Purposes

We use this information to run quizzes and comparisons, deliver messages, enforce message limits, keep the service safe (including handling reports and blocks), and respond to requests.

6. Service providers

Backend services are provided by Supabase. In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), and we verify them with those stores. Google AdMob may be used for advertising, including rewarded ads you choose to watch; ad providers may process device and advertising information under their own policies and your consent choices. On iOS, tracking is only used if you allow it.

7. Sharing

Sharing a result or invitation is optional and opens your device sharing interface. The app never publishes your results automatically. In shared quizzes, the other participant sees only the match score, not your individual answers.

8. Storage, retention and security

Records are stored in Supabase and protected by database access policies. Invitation codes expire after 7 days; invitations nobody joined are deleted shortly after. Records remain until you delete your account, except report evidence as described above. Backups may take additional time to expire.

9. Your rights and deletion

Tap Delete my account below to permanently delete your guest account, quiz history, shared quiz answers, chat data on our servers and chat messages stored on this device. You may also request access, correction or deletion by emailing mertkandemir01@icloud.com with the subject VibeCheck Data Request.

10. Children and entertainment

Quiz results are for entertainment, not medical or psychological assessment. Do not share sensitive personal information in quizzes, chats or support requests.

11. Updates

This notice may change when features or service providers change. The updated text and effective date will be shown in the app.
