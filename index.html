Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to take a quiz?

VibeCheck is designed for guest use. Quizzes can be completed without creating a traditional email/password account. A guest session may be created to save your quiz result.

Are quiz results a psychological assessment?

No. VibeCheck results are created for entertainment and self-expression, not medical or psychological diagnosis.

How do I share my result?

Finish a quiz, choose a result-card theme, and select “Share my vibe.” Sharing uses your device's sharing options. If the image-sharing feature is unavailable, text sharing may be offered instead.

Can I change the app language?

The app interface offers English, Turkish, German, Spanish, Italian, and French. Some sample quiz content may remain in English until translation work is complete.

What if a quiz or sharing feature does not work?

Try closing and reopening the app, checking your network connection, and installing the latest available version. If the problem continues, include your device model, operating system, app version, and a description of what happened in your support request. Never send passwords or sensitive information.

How do I request data deletion or ask about privacy?

Read the Privacy Policy. You can request deletion in the app under Privacy Policy → Delete my account, or email mertkandemir01@icloud.com with the subject “VibeCheck Data Request”.

Can I compare results with a friend or partner?

Friend and couple quiz categories are present in the prototype. Secure invitations and comparison sessions are still under development.

Contact support

For bug reports, accessibility questions, privacy requests, or account deletion assistance, contact VibeCheck support at mertkandemir01@icloud.com.

Email VibeCheck support

When contacting support, describe the issue and what you expected to happen. Please do not include sensitive personal information.
