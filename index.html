Frequently asked questions

Do I need to register?

Not for Discover Yourself or Friends: the app uses an anonymous guest session. Couple Match needs Sign in with Apple or Google, so you meet real people; your quiz history comes with you.

Are the results scientific?

No. All results are for entertainment only.

How do I share a result?

Complete a quiz and tap Share my vibe to use your device’s sharing options.

How do I play with a friend or partner?

Choose Friends, tap Invite & play together and share the link or code. You both answer separately; the comparison unlocks when both finish.

How does Couple Match work?

Choose Couples and tap Find a match. You are matched with someone new who is online at the same time and whose preferences fit yours both ways. You answer the same quiz, see your compatibility and, from a 70% basic match and if you both agree, you can chat. You can decline, leave or block at any time.

What are the Couple Match extra packs?

After the second-to-last question you can both choose: see the free result, unlock more questions with one rewarded ad, or unlock the deep test with a one-time purchase. A pack starts only if you both choose it; only one of you watches or pays. The extra questions give a separate detailed score and never change your basic result.

Can I change the language?

Yes, with the language button on the home screen. Some quiz content may still be available only in English.

Someone is bothering me in chat.

Long-press the message to report it, or block the person from the chat menu. Blocking closes the chat immediately.

What if something breaks?

Restart the app, check your connection and send your device model, app version and a description of the issue.

How do I delete my data?

Open Privacy Policy and tap Delete my account, or email the support address with the subject VibeCheck Data Request.

Contact support

For a bug report, accessibility issue, safety report, privacy question or deletion request, email us. Please describe the issue and what you expected to happen, and do not include sensitive personal information.
