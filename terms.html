Community Terms

Please read these terms before using chat.

1. Adults only

Couple Match is only for people aged 18 and over. Accounts that appear to belong to minors are removed.

2. Be respectful

You can decline any chat invitation; a decline is final for that match and the other person only sees a kind preset message. Do not try to contact someone again after they declined or blocked you.

3. Zero tolerance

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. You must not send harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content, spam, or content that is illegal or violates someone else’s rights.

4. Filtering

Messages are automatically checked against a list of prohibited terms and may be rejected before delivery.

5. Reporting and blocking

You can report any message you receive and block the sender at any time. Blocking closes the chat for both people.

6. Enforcement

Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. We remove offending content and permanently remove access for users who break these terms.

7. Entertainment only

Compatibility scores are just for fun and are not advice of any kind.
